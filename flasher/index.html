MeshCore CYD flasher

Flash a MeshCore CYD colour-UI build straight from the browser. Use desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial); Firefox and Safari are not supported.


Your browser can't do Web Serial - use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Serial access was blocked - allow it and retry.

The installer erases the chip first, so touch calibration and the node identity are reset. On first boot the device shows the touch-calibration screen - tap the four corner markers.

Changing a board's role (companion ↔ repeater) is a full erase - fine here, the installer already erases. Powering the board from a PC USB port while it transmits at 30 dBm can brown the port out; a powered hub or external 5 V is steadier.

Images come from dist/ in the repo. Prefer a cable and a flashing tool you trust? Every image is also a plain esptool write_flash 0x0 <file>.
